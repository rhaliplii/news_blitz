News Blitz

React Fast or Miss the Move!

Test your market intuition by reacting to breaking financial news. Is it Bullish 📈 or Bearish 📉? The faster you respond, the higher your score!

iHow to Play

  • 📰You’ll see a breaking news headline
  • ⚡Tap “Bullish” 📈 or “Bearish” 📉 within the time limit
  • ❤️Correct answers earn points, wrong answers cost lives
  • ⏱️In Survival Mode, the clock gets faster as you progress!

    Survival Mode

    In Survival Mode, time speeds up, and you only have 3 lives. Can you survive?

    • You start with 2 lives
    • Each mistake costs 1 life
    • Time per question decreases as you progress, down to 1.0s
    • Every 10 correct answers in a row = +1 life (max 3)
    • +10 Coins every 5 correct in a row
    • +50 Coins for a 20 streak, +50 for every 60s survived
